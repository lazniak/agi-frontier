Every flagship model, one index, and an honest guess at what comes next.

Labs publish benchmark scores in a different mix every time, so raw percentages are not comparable. AGI Frontier fits a single 0–100 Frontier Index per model from the numbers each lab reported itself — not an average, but the latent ability of a Rasch model, drawn on a logit axis so a step from 90 to 97 is as large as one from 50 to 80. It then models each lab's release cadence to forecast when — and how capable — the next flagship will be. Everything on this page is recomputed in your browser from one auditable file, back to the first GPT.

    Method

    A flagship is the most capable tier a lab offers at launch; mini, flash and lite tiers are excluded. Only numbers a lab published itself count as official, and every score carries a URL and a verbatim quote that a worker re-checks. Averaging raw percentages would reward a model for reporting only easy benchmarks, so instead a one-parameter logistic (Rasch) model is fitted jointly over every official score: logit(score) = θmodel − δbenchmark. The Frontier Index is 100·σ(θ) — the score a model would be expected to get on a benchmark of average difficulty in the basket — so it is comparable even when two models reported different subsets. A hollow marker — matched by a "provisional" badge in the rankings and the release watch — means the release reported fewer than three index benchmarks, so its index rests on too little evidence to form the frontier line or feed a lab's trend, and it is listed after the qualified flagships. Release timing comes from a log-normal fit of each lab's inter-release intervals, shrunk toward the cross-lab prior so a lab with two releases does not get absurd certainty. The yellow circle is centred on the median predicted date and its diameter is the 16th–84th percentile window; the fan is the 10th–90th percentile of extrapolated capability. Scrubbing back in time re-runs the whole fit as of that date, which is how you check whether the forecast would have called what actually happened.
